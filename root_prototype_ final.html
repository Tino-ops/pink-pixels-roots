About ROOTS

We wanted to make it easier for people to discover Black Canadian history without having to search through a bunch of different websites. The idea behind ROOTS is to bring topics together in one place, while still giving credit to the museums, archives and organizations that hold the original information. Right now, this is a browser-based demo using example data. It isn't connected to live archives or AI yet.
